# Making History 1 & Gold Editor 4.0

- **Islands added to an island region could not be clicked in game.** Island regions keep a drawn mesh and a separate click area; area added to them only went into the drawn one. It now goes into both, and **Validate** flags regions already affected with a **Make it selectable** button.
- `tools/idioma_juego.py` (game language): also reads and writes `HKCU\Software\Muzzy Lane Software\<game>\Locale`, which the game reads before HKLM and VirtualStore; before, changing the language could have no effect.
- **One «⇪ Import from another game…» button** in the scenario editor replaces «⇪ From MH2». It asks for the source game and opens that game's importer:
  - **Making History II**: unchanged (WorldMoreRegions / WorldMoreRegionsRivers).
  - **Hearts of Iron IV** (new): creates an MH1 scenario on the WorldHOI4 regionpack from the game or a mod that keeps HOI4's map, at the 1936 or 1939 bookmark (dated history blocks applied up to that date). It copies state owners, population (from manpower), cities (victory points), capitals, factories as IPUs, naval bases as ports, resources (oil; steel, aluminium, tungsten and chromium as metals; HOI4 has no coal), country colours, governments (ruling party), names from the localisation and flags. Countries named by MH1's shared events take their MH1 name (German Reich → Germany, Soviet Union → USSR…), and World regions named by those events are replaced by the WorldHOI4 region at the same place (Muscovy → Moscow). Units, research, national focuses and diplomacy are not imported.
  - **Pax Historia** (new): Pax Historia has no export, so the dialog gives a short browser-console snippet that saves the open preset as `pax_<id>.json`. The importer downloads the preset's public map geometry and lays its political map over an MH1 scenario you choose; population, cities, resources, units, research and diplomacy come from that base scenario. Each land region goes to the Pax country covering most of it, on any regionpack (projected ones such as WorldHOI4 included). Countries the base already has are reused by name (The German Reich → Germany, Kingdom of Italy → Italy) or by shared land (Imperial State of Iran → Persia). The others are created with their Pax colour and flag, and base countries left without land are removed. Countries without a city get a capital in their biggest region, and the start date is the preset's.
  - **Making History: The Great War** and **Making History: The Second World War** (new): their scenarios (game and Workshop) convert onto WorldMoreRegions / WorldMoreRegionsRivers like MH2's. Both games share a map of their own; each region goes into the WorldMoreRegions region of the same name or the one covering most of its shape (AlsaceLorraine → Alsace, Azov → Rostov…), cities into the region that contains them. Government types become MH1 governments (monarchies and dictatorships → Authoritarian, republics → Democratic, socialist/fascist states → Communist/Fascist), WWI-era units take the oldest matching MH1 unit type of the template (pre-dreadnoughts → PreWarBattleship, howitzers and mortars → FieldArtillery); balloons, airships, colliers and seaplane tenders have no MH1 equivalent and are skipped. States the game's shared events know by another name take it (Kingdom of Italy → Italy, Ottoman → Turkey, Soviet Union → USSR). Colonies and protectorates become part of their mother country (MH1 has no such status) and puppets are not playable. Flags are the games' own period flags (MH2's set as a fallback). Converted scenarios get no title/button images instead of the template's. MH1's standard shared events that name a country or region the converted scenario lacks (the 1936 Axis/Allies eligibility, USSR or Italy near defeat, Yugoslav coup… in a 1914 scenario) are removed together with their decisions and triggers.
  - **New regionpack WorldTGW** (MH1 and Gold): the map of The Great War / The Second World War, built from WorldMoreRegionsMH2 by changing only what differs from MH2 (`tools/tgw_make_pack.py`): 30 regions renamed (Armenia → ArmenianHighland, Kalinin → Rzhevsky, NorthernIreland → Ulster…), 76 new regions (AlsaceLorraine, Ruhr, Azov, Chelyabinsk…), 33 MH2 regions merged into the TGW one that covers them, and 497 outlines reshaped to TGW's. TGW/SWW lakes stay as in MH2. The TGW and SWW importers use it when it is installed (regions match by name), otherwise they fall back to WorldMoreRegions.
  - The MH2 / TGW / SWW importers no longer delete the official template scenarios' `ScenarioDefaults` shared data files when dropping the template's events (they only drop the reference), regions now get their MH2 culture, and starting wars work in a scenario that has no alliance yet.
  - If the game's regionpack is not installed but its editor project is, the importer offers to install it.
- Deleting a nation also removes the other nations' AI reactions to it (`TriggerCountry`) and their starting relations with it (`DipRelInitialValue`); before, **Validate** reported them as naming a country that does not exist.
- The map editor no longer checks the Windows registry for an old game license at startup or offers to delete it (the «Invalid License Key» fixer); `tools/licencia_juego.py` still works from the command line.
- «Redraw borders» style names shortened to «MH1 borders» / «MH2 borders».
- Wikidata importer: when a country has no air-force roundel, it shows its coat of arms (P94) with «Import coat of arms as roundel…».
- The editors find Making History 1 and Gold installed outside Steam's default folder: on external disks, without Steam (the game's own registry entries), or in the usual folders of every drive. If one still isn't found, «MH1 folder…» / «Gold folder…» in the game selector lets you point to it; the choice is remembered.
- **Events can no longer rename a region or change its culture.** Those effects used ActAlterProperty on the region; the game rejects it inside an event («Can't find player while executing ActAlterProperty»), so the choice is not executed, the decision window can't be closed and the turn stays stuck (The Modern World Reloaded's Malvinas event). The «Change the culture of regions» effect is gone from the event editor, and **Validate** shows an error for events that still change a property this way.
- Scenario editor, nation panel: new «Nation cultures» box to tick the nation's cultures (its CultureSet), as in the official editor's nation dialog.
- Scenario editor, nation panel: new «Starting stockpile» box with each resource's starting amount (Coal, Oil, Metals, Food, Armaments, Consumer goods), as the official editor's «Stockpiles»; empty removes it.
- Scenario editor, unit templates (sharedDataUnitTemplates.xml): new sheet like the official editor's, with ranged number fields (hit points and speed ≥ 1, hit percentages and resistance 0–100, the rest ≥ 0), type and category dropdowns, a checkbox list of capabilities and the air-damage / air-squadron flags. **Validate** now also flags an invalid type or category, negative men or range and Gold's land-defense hit percent outside 0–100.
- Scenario editor, productions (sharedDataFactories.xml): new sheet for unit, factory and resource outputs with tech level, IPUs, MPUs, build cost, port-only, available-by-default, display type, the unit template or resource it produces and its required resources (add, change, remove). **Validate** now flags an unknown unit template (as an error), an unknown produced resource, an invalid display type and negative MPUs; resource-requirement problems open the production itself.
- Scenario editor, research projects (sharedDataResearch.xml): new sheet with cost, IPUs, research points, branch, program, description and effect texts, completed-by-default, prerequisites, unlocked and retired productions, unit upgrades and the old → new template of template upgrades. **Validate** now flags unknown prerequisites (as errors), missing description or effect texts, template types or names that don't exist and unknown unit upgrades; each problem opens the project's sheet with «Edit».
- New cities, capitals and resources placed «in a region» without coordinates (and objects moved to another region) now go to a free spot of the region, away from its cities and resources. Before, every object of a region got the same point, so a resource or a second city landed right on top of the existing city.
- New armies, air forces and fleets (not in port) get a position in a free spot of their region, away from cities, resources and other units; before they had none and the game drew them on the region's city.
- Making nations playable («Playable nations») now also sets the nation's own type (MECountry eCountryType = COUNTRYTYPE_PLAYABLE, bNonPlayable) and its Player in gamestate.xml; before, a nation made playable only in scenarioInfo.xml could be chosen but was left out of the in-game score list.
- Nation colours between MH1 and Gold: «Export to Gold…/MH1…» has a checkbox (off by default) to convert colours, giving each nation the colour it has in the target game's official scenarios (otherwise the closest equivalent). New **Gold palette** / **MH1 palette** buttons in the scenario editor's Colors tab do the same to the open scenario; the change is written on «Save».
- **Embargo events no longer fail.** The embargo effect and the «protection» reaction now say who embargoes (strPlayer on ActSetTradeRelations); without it the game logged «Couldn't find ID of player country», the decision failed every turn and a game could lock at turn 2. **Validate** shows an error for embargo actions without strPlayer.
- Scenario editor: new **«War / embargo reactions»** tab, next to «Player / AI», listing every AI reaction of the scenario (`TriggerCountry`) in one place.
  - Each row shows who reacts, an attack on which country it reacts to, and how likely a war or an embargo against the aggressor is (0–100, also in words: never, rarely, sometimes, likely, almost certain). A summary counts the reactions at each level.
  - Pick a row to change it with sliders and a sentence that says what it will do. You can also move it to another country, add a new one (optionally both ways) or remove it.
  - «See a country from both sides» lists what a nation does when others are attacked, and who reacts when it is attacked.
  - «Change many at once» works on every reaction the list shows (filter it to pick a country): multiply, cap, set or add to the war or embargo value, or remove them.
  - The AI section of the nation panel links to the tab.
- «Create units in bulk» no longer hands out nuclear units. A country without an air force could get «AtomicBombers», the cheapest air template the scenario allowed, so small countries started the game with atomic bombs. Templates that can drop a nuke are now skipped, unless the country already has them.
- Scenario editor, nation panel: new «Troop appearance» box (unit 3D models, MKDTableCountryAliasesLand/Air.xml). «Same troop appearance as…» copies another country's land and/or air models, and «Change unit by unit» picks the model of each unit from the ones the scenario already uses. «＋ New → Nation» has a «Troops like» choice; left on automatic, the new nation takes the models of its template or of the country its regions (or capital) came from. Deleting a nation removes its models; renaming one already renamed them.
- Map editor, «Cut»: tiny loose exclaves left by earlier cuts (needles under 1e-8 degrees²) inside the cut polygon now move to the target region; before, «Cut» dropped them as border crumbs and they could never be removed.
- `tools/build_exe.py --dist <folder>` builds MHEditor.exe in another folder (e.g. a faster disk); a `mh_root.txt` next to the exe points to the data folder.
- Map editor, «Import GeoJSON»: an outline named like a region elsewhere on the map (e.g. Argentina's «Formosa» vs Formosa in Taiwan) is no longer taken as a duplicate. Before, accepting the duplicates warning deleted the unrelated region. Now the outline is skipped with a note asking you to rename it.
- Map editor, region panel: «＋ Add area from OSM relation…» and «－ Subtract area from OSM relation…» take the outline straight from an OpenStreetMap relation id or link, simplified like «Import GeoJSON».
- MH1 ⇄ Gold converter: it now fixes what kept converted scenarios from loading. Productions retired by two research projects get `bMayAlreadyBeUnavailable` (before, the game aborted research setup), `fileMapProperties` follows `strMapName`, an empty `strMapViewsName` becomes `UnifiedMapViews` (before, the map had no political fill or icons), and sprites used by the scenario's MKD tables but missing from its own sprite libraries are copied from the target game. The dialog explains each step, and the loading bar shows the real percentage and the current step.
