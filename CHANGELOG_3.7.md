# Making History 1 & Gold Editor 3.7

- Scenario editor: «Bulk units…» → «Stacked groups: Merge…» merges the armies, air forces and fleets of one country that sit on the same spot (same region and position, or no position, which the game draws all on one point) into a single group with all their units. Groups named by an event are never removed.
- Russian interface: choose «Русский» in the language selector (all editor texts and the window menu). Translation checks now also report texts missing in Russian.
- Every GeoJSON picker also accepts KML and KMZ (Google Earth): new regions, add/subtract area, island painting, background outline and the scenario editor's «Import GeoJSON…»; the scenario import and the import dialog also bring KML pins in as points (cities, units…).
- AI assistant (🤖 AI button, draggable; first version): give orders in plain words in the region, scenario, image and 3D model editors («create an infantry army for Spain in Madrid», «war event Spain → Portugal on 01/01/2026», «import this KML and give it to Poland»). The AI proposes the whole plan, you approve it once and the editor applies it; changes stay unsaved until you press Save, and a copy of the scenario is made before the first change. Works with your own API key: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral, Qwen, Kimi, GLM and more, any OpenAI-compatible API, or local models (Ollama, LM Studio); the model list comes from each API, with fallback providers if one fails. Keys stay only on your computer. Chats can be saved, reopened and deleted.
